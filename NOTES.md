First packaged release of Vox Pro.

- 8 inputs and 8 outputs (A1-A5, B1-B3) with a routing matrix
- **Parametric EQ** on every strip and every output: six cells with a curve you can drag
- **Plugin rack:** LV2 plugins (LSP, Calf, x42...) on any strip or output
- Compressor on every strip, limiter on every output
- Record any output to FLAC, WAV or Opus
- Built-in updater
